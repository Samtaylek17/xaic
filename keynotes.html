Keynote Talk I · 02:15PM–03:00PM

[WiP] A New Paradigm of Multicultural Safety Evaluations: Benchmarking Explicit and Implicit Cultural Context Across Modalities

Francesca Federico

Google DeepMind

Francesca Federico is a Red Teaming Lead at Google DeepMind, where her work sits at the intersection of technology, AI safety, and societal impact. She is a sociotechnical researcher that has spent the last several years red-teaming frontier AI models - evaluating both adversarial robustness to misuse risks, such as CBRNe, and emerging societal harms. She is particularly interested in exploring the second-order effects of AI, advocating for a responsible approach to evaluating these systems with a specific focus on assessing macroeconomic allocative harms.

Keynote Talk II · 03:15PM–04:00PM

REACH GenAI: Community-Informed Evaluation and Benchmarking of Culturally Competent Generative AI

Andrew Smart

Google

Andrew Smart is a Senior Research Scientist at Google, specializing in responsible AI, machine learning fairness, and AI governance. His interdisciplinary background spans anthropology, philosophy, cognitive science, neuroscience, and machine learning. His research focuses on the societal implications of artificial intelligence, including algorithmic fairness, accountability, transparency, explainability, and the evaluation of AI-related risks. Before joining Google, he held research and technical positions at organizations including Twitter, Novartis, and Honeywell Aerospace. His work aims to advance the development and evaluation of responsible, trustworthy, and socially beneficial AI systems..
